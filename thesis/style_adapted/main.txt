ABSTRACT
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

The growing availability of statistical data in professional football has reshaped how matches are analysed, yet most existing analytics tools remain focused on outcome forecasting. In a real coaching environment, however, the decision problem is not only \emph{what} is likely to happen, but also \emph{what should be done} to improve the chance of a favourable result. This thesis addresses that gap by investigating the transition from \emph{predictive} to \emph{prescriptive} analytics in football and by proposing \textbf{UmbraRo}, a coach-oriented tactical-assistance system built on top of a custom machine learning framework for the Romanian Superliga.

To capture the dynamics of a fixture, the prediction task is reformulated from the classical three-class home-win / draw / away-win setting to a more stable binary classification problem, \emph{Home Win} versus \emph{Not Home Win}. The analytical backbone of the system is a dataset of approximately 1{,}600 Romanian Superliga matches spanning five seasons (2020--2021 to 2024--2025), with a strict chronological split that reserves the 2024--2025 season as a holdout test period. A set of engineered pre-match features is constructed, including computed Elo ratings, head-to-head context, rest days, and rolling five-match averages of possession, shots, shots on target, corners, goals scored, and goals conceded. Seven supervised classifiers are compared (Logistic Regression, Random Forest, XGBoost, CatBoost, Support Vector Machines, Naive Bayes, and Multi-Layer Perceptrons), evaluated through rolling-origin validation and judged by both classification metrics (accuracy, F1) and probability-quality metrics (Brier score and Expected Calibration Error).

The experimental results expose a meaningful gap between predictive performance and prescriptive usefulness. Logistic Regression achieved the strongest pure predictive accuracy ($64.89\%$), while calibrated CatBoost obtained a Brier score of $0.2329$ and an Expected Calibration Error of $0.0517$. Embedded inside a tactical optimisation loop, however, Logistic Regression reacted too aggressively to perturbations of tactical inputs, whereas CatBoost produced smoother and more realistic probability responses. For example, the constrained Monte Carlo optimiser at $N = 25{,}000$ samples increased the estimated home-win probability from $22.10\%$ to $30.76\%$ in UTA Arad versus FCSB ($+8.7$ percentage points) and from $37.82\%$ to $44.14\%$ in CFR Cluj versus FCSB ($+6.3$ percentage points), averaged across five random seeds. On this basis, CatBoost was retained as the production prescriptive engine, while Logistic Regression was kept as a benchmark for pure forecasting.

Beyond the analytical framework, a major contribution of this thesis is the practical deployment of the resulting models. The predictive and prescriptive layers are exposed to end users through UmbraRo, a Flutter web/mobile application backed by a Python FastAPI service. The system surfaces, for every upcoming fixture, the calibrated baseline win probability, the optimised probability returned by a \emph{constrained Monte Carlo tactical optimiser}, the most influential positive and negative drivers, a numeric tactical blueprint, and a recommended starting eleven. This combination of forecasting, explanation, and prescription is delivered through the \emph{Match Intelligence and Tactical Blueprint} module, which is the on-screen embodiment of the academic contributions of the thesis.

A central finding of this work is that, in a football setting, forecast accuracy and tactical realism behave as distinct optimisation criteria rather than as a single shared quality, and a coach-facing system should be designed around the latter. By coupling a calibrated probabilistic classifier with a constrained Monte Carlo optimiser, UmbraRo turns probabilistic forecasting into an operational decision-support tool, demonstrating that prescriptive analytics under domain-specific constraints is a viable foundation for tactical assistance in professional football.
