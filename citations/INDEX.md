## How to use this archive

For thesis defence:
- A committee member asks "where exactly does Lahvička say 12 EPL seasons?" → open `citations/lahvicka2015importance.pdf` and search for "twelve" or "12".
- A claim in §2.3 needs reverification → the PDF is one click away.

For continued thesis writing:
- Citation keys in `references.bib` match filenames in `citations/` exactly, no remembering required.
- The 9 entries that require manual action are listed above with the manual-fallback URL.
